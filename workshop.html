Building data governance that sticks

A turnkey virtual-workshop sample with a facilitator guide, slide deck and differentiated participant worksheet.

One 60-minute session, with separate pathways for foundational and established city teams.

Design rationale

The learner
Mixed-experience groups need different levels of scaffolding.
The approach
Use backward design: diagnose one problem, practice in a maturity-appropriate peer group, then commit to a next step.
The intended outcome
A facilitator can inspect timings, scripts and contingencies. Partner approval or live delivery has not been established.

Choose a learning track

Track A: building foundations

Use this pathway when ownership, an inventory or a convening group is missing.

  1. Name one urgent resident problem.
  2. Identify the two departments and three datasets most relevant to it.
  3. Write a 30-day action and name the role needed to help.
Track B: scaling and optimizing

Use this pathway when a governance structure already exists.

  1. Diagnose a recurring agreement, equity or transition bottleneck.
  2. Choose a process that could be standardized safely.
  3. Define what success would look like in six months.

Partner delivery notes

Every transition has an intended cue. If a track attracts fewer than two people, form a mixed-experience coaching room. If time slips, use chat for the debrief and protect the final commitment.

For consecutive interpretation, plan 75 minutes: extend the breakout clinic from 17 to 25 minutes, debrief from 10 to 15 minutes, and reserve two extra minutes for transitions.

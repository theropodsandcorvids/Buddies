Design rationale

The learner
Procurement guidance can bury the practical next step in legal and technical language.
The approach
Organize the supplied sample around three actions, a concrete scoring table and common objections.
The intended outcome
A concise meeting reference. The source manual and claimed 85% reading-time reduction were not supplied.

Quick-start guide

Download quick-start guide (PDF)
  1. Define the resident outcomeWho benefits? What changes? What is the starting point?
  2. Assess vendor capacityUse criteria permitted by the applicable procurement rules.
  3. Review performance togetherAgree data, meeting cadence and written change control.

These are teaching weights, not a required rubric or verified WWC standard.

Behind the synthesis

What changed and why
  • Convert scoring prose into a table so a reviewer can compare categories quickly.
  • Add a low-tech starting option so small teams do not assume they need a data platform.
  • Keep common objections beside practical responses. Legal applicability still needs local review.
